SecurityRelief Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026.

Who we are

SecurityRelief is a product of Syneraz (Commercial Registration 7054370049). Syneraz is the company responsible for the data we collect on this site.

What we collect before you start the assessment

Before you start the assessment, the only thing we ask you for is your mobile number. Like most sites, we also automatically record your IP address, your browser and device type, and how you reached us, including the link we sent you if you came from one of our emails.

What we collect in the assessment and the report form

During the assessment: we save your answers and the score calculated from them. On the report form at the end of the assessment: we save your full name, job title, company name, work email, number of employees, sector if you choose one, and the time you agreed to the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy. We do not ask for any sensitive personal data.

If you submit a LinkedIn lead form for one of our ads, LinkedIn shares your name, work email, company and job title with us. We use them only to send you the assessment link and to contact you about it.

How we use the data

We use your mobile number to send you the verification code by SMS, to make sure a real person is starting the assessment. We use your answers to calculate your score, and the form details to prepare your report and send it to your work email. Our team sees your details and your score, and may contact you about your assessment by email or on your mobile number.

Who receives your data

We do not sell your data. Part of it reaches companies we use to run the service and measure how it performs. A Saudi SMS provider receives your mobile number to deliver the verification code. An email provider delivers your report to your work email, and delivers to our team a notice containing the form details, your mobile number, your score, some of your answers and a copy of your report. We may keep your record with the provider of the customer records system we use, including the form details, your mobile number and your score. If you book a session with us, you enter your details yourself on a booking page run by the booking provider we use, and we receive them to arrange your appointment. Google and LinkedIn receive the visit data described in the last section.

Where your data is kept and for how long

The site and its database run on a cloud server. The notice that reaches our team, with a copy of your report, stays in the team's email inbox. We do not delete your data automatically: we keep it until you ask us to delete it, except the web server's technical log (IP address and the links of pages requested), where the oldest entries are deleted automatically. Your result page and your report open with the link alone, with no password: anyone who has the link can open them, so share it only with people you trust.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time, in line with Saudi Arabia's Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL), and you can ask us to stop contacting you. Email us at info@syneraz.com and tell us the mobile number or work email you used, so we can find your record. We will respond within 14 days. Deleting your data with us does not delete what our SMS provider, our email provider, Google and LinkedIn have already received, or what is already noted in the web server's technical log, or the nightly backup copies on our server, which are deleted automatically after about two weeks.

Cookies and measurement tools

We use two cookies of our own: one keeps the language you choose, the other records how you reached us. We also use two third-party measurement tools that run from the moment the page opens: Google Analytics, to measure visits, and LinkedIn's advertising tool (Insight Tag), to measure how our advertising performs. Both save files of their own in your browser and receive your IP address, how you reached us, and the links of the pages you open, including the link to your result page. Google Analytics also receives a signal each time you complete a step on the site.
